I've pushed through things I wasn't sure I could do and discovered I had more in me than I thought.

I've also convinced myself I wasn't ready, qualified, or capable enough and let that belief stop me.

BEYOND is where I'm working through both experiences.

These aren't instructions for living, and I certainly don't have all the answers. They're reflections on things I'm still learning myself. Maybe something here will cause you to question an assumption of your own.

This isn't about believing anything is possible.

It's about being a little more careful when deciding what isn't.

Why I'm Writing This

I don't have this figured out.

I'm an educator, Marine veteran, husband, father, grandfather, and lifelong student. Different parts of my life have repeatedly put me in situations where I wasn't entirely sure I was ready for what came next.

Sometimes I surprised myself.

Sometimes I failed.

Sometimes I learned, adjusted, and tried again.

And sometimes I let the possibility of failure keep me from trying in the first place.

I've become increasingly interested in that space between what we know we can do and what we haven't yet discovered that we can do.

That's where BEYOND came from.

I'm not writing because I've figured it all out. I'm writing because I haven't.

We're all living within boundaries.

Some protect us.

Some reflect the things and people we value.

Some are real.

And some may deserve another look.
