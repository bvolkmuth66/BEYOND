About BEYOND

Not motivational certainty. Honest experimentation.

BEYOND is a series about questioning the boundaries we accept without pretending every limit can or should be defeated. It is interested in preparation, responsibility, discomfort, learning, recovery, adaptation, and the evidence we gain by trying.

The project is written by Brian Volkmuth and published as a collection of short web essays designed to be read, shared, and reflected on.
