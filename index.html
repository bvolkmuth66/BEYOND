What if some of the limits we live within aren't really ours?

Thoughts on resilience, growth, and discovering what we're capable of.

We don't always know what we're capable of until something asks us to find out.

Explore BEYOND ↓

I've pushed through things I wasn't sure I could do and discovered I had more in me than I thought. I've also convinced myself I wasn't ready, qualified, or capable enough and let that belief stop me.

BEYOND is where I'm working through both experiences. These aren't instructions for living. They're reflections on things I'm still learning myself.

Why I'm Writing This

I don't have this figured out.

I'm an educator, Marine veteran, husband, father, grandfather, and lifelong student. I've had successes, failures, and plenty of moments when I wasn't sure I was ready for whatever came next.

I'm not writing because I've figured it all out. I'm writing because I haven't.

A Final Thought

We're all living within boundaries.

Some protect us. Some reflect the things and people we value. Some are real.

And some may deserve another look.
